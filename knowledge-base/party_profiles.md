# Party Profiles — Companion Characters

## Companion 1: Bram Ashford — Warden

### Role
Frontline fighter, tracker, survival specialist

### Physical Description
Late thirties. Built like someone who has been doing hard physical work 
since childhood. Short dark hair going grey at the temples. A scar runs 
from his left ear to his jaw — he tells people it was a corrupted wolf. 
It was not a wolf. Wears practical layered armor with Guild markings that 
have been re-stitched so many times the fabric around them is thicker 
than the original.

### Personality
Tough, reliable, economical with words. Bram does not say anything he 
does not mean and does not mean anything he will not back up. He has 
been a Warden for sixteen years and has survived things that killed 
better operatives than him by being consistently competent rather than 
occasionally brilliant. He is not inspiring. He is exactly what you 
want next to you when something goes wrong.

### Background
Born in a frontier settlement that was destroyed in a Rifting when he 
was twelve. Was pulled out of the rubble by a Warden and has worked for 
the Guild ever since. He does not talk about the settlement or what he 
saw. He does not need to. Anyone who knows frontier history knows which 
Rifting he means.

### How He Meets The Player
Sitting alone at the far end of the Ashen Flagon, working through a bowl 
of something grey and unidentifiable. Nods when the player sits down. 
Does not introduce himself first. If the player is heading into the 
Greymark he will say he is going the same direction and it makes more 
sense to travel together. That is the entirety of his pitch.

### Capabilities
- Tracking and survival in the Greymark
- Melee combat, weapon proficiency
- Creature identification and behavior prediction
- Emergency field medicine (basic, not Thorncraft)

### Disposition Toward Player at First Meeting
Neutral. Cautious respect. He has worked with enough new operatives to 
know not to form opinions before he has seen them work.

### Secrets (GM Only)
Bram has been inside the Greymark further than any active Warden on 
record. Three years ago he reached the edge of what he now believes 
was Drenhold territory and turned back. He has never filed a report 
about what he saw there. He recognized the symbol on Ashwatch Post's 
wall immediately when he heard the description. He has seen it before.

## Companion 2: Lysa Vorn — Runescribe

### Role
Arcane scholar, rune specialist, knowledge retrieval

### Physical Description
Mid twenties. Slight, precise in her movements. Dark eyes that move 
constantly, reading everything in a room before she speaks. Her forearms 
are covered in faded rune inscriptions — practice marks from years of 
training, some permanent, some partially faded. Carries three satchels 
at all times. She knows exactly what is in each one.

### Personality
Curious, methodical, occasionally oblivious to social context because 
she is thinking about something more interesting. Lysa is not cold — 
she is just operating on a different frequency. When something catches 
her attention she becomes intensely focused. When nothing does she is 
quietly cataloguing everything around her for future reference. She 
asks questions constantly, including ones people find uncomfortable.

### Background
Trained at the Runescribe collegium in the Kingdom of Valdenmoor. Left 
before completing her senior certification after a disagreement with her 
senior instructor about whether Blight-touched materials could theoretically 
be used as a substrate for rune inscription. The collegium said no. She 
said the question deserved proper investigation. She is in Greyhold 
because it is the closest place to enough Blight-adjacent material to 
actually test her theory.

### How She Meets The Player
At a corner table in the Ashen Flagon with three books open simultaneously 
and a cup of tea that has gone cold. If the player mentions anything 
related to the Greymark, the missing outposts, or unusual creature 
behavior she will look up from her books and insert herself into the 
conversation without being invited. She will not apologize for this.

### Capabilities
- Rune inscription — offensive, defensive, and utility effects
- Blight analysis — identifying corruption levels, seam proximity, 
  creature mutation stage
- Historical and arcane knowledge retrieval
- Pre-prepared rune items for specific scenarios

### Disposition Toward Player at First Meeting
Interested but evaluating. She wants to know if the player is going 
somewhere useful. If they are she wants to come. If they are not she 
will go back to her books.

### Secrets (GM Only)
Lysa's theory about Blight-touched rune substrates is correct and she 
knows it is correct because she has already tested it once. The result 
was a rune of unusual power that also left a grey stain on her left 
palm that she keeps covered with a glove. She does not know yet whether 
the stain is purely cosmetic. Signe the Thornwarden does know and has 
been trying to find a moment to tell her.

## Companion 3: Edda Voss — Thornwarden

### Role
Healer, Blight specialist, field support

### Physical Description
Early forties. Weathered in the way of someone who has spent years 
outdoors in bad conditions. White hair cut short and practical. Her 
hands are permanently stained a faint grey-green from years of handling 
Thorncraft materials — no amount of washing removes it at this point. 
She wears a grey cloak with no Guild markings. Thornwardens are not 
officially Guild members. They operate alongside the Guild by informal 
arrangement and mutual necessity.

### Personality
Quiet, observant, unhurried. Edda does not perform her confidence — 
she simply has it. She has seen enough of what the Blight does to 
people to have made her peace with the uncomfortable realities of her 
work. She does not explain herself to people who have not earned the 
right to an explanation. She is not cruel about this. She just does 
not need external validation for choices she has already examined 
thoroughly.

### Background
Was a conventional healer in a frontier settlement for fifteen years 
before a Rifting left her with thirty casualties and no conventional 
medicine capable of treating Blight corruption. She learned Thorncraft 
from the only person in the region who knew it — an old woman who 
lived alone at the Greymark edge and was regarded locally as a witch. 
Edda does not consider that characterization entirely wrong. She has 
been practicing for twelve years. She is very good.

### How She Meets The Player
Outside the Ashen Flagon, finishing a conversation with a Warden who 
is trying to pretend he does not need the poultice she is holding out 
to him. When the player passes she looks at them with the particular 
attention of someone who has learned to read how much time people have 
left. She will not say what she sees. If the player is heading toward 
the Greymark she will fall into step and say she is going that way 
anyway. She is not going that way anyway. She wants to see what is 
happening at Ashwatch Post for her own reasons.

### Capabilities
- Thorncraft healing — treats wounds, Blight corruption, and conditions 
  conventional medicine cannot address
- Blight resistance preparation — pre-journey treatments that slow 
  corruption exposure
- Creature behavior analysis from a biological perspective
- Emergency field surgery and stabilization

### Disposition Toward Player at First Meeting
Neutral, watchful. She is deciding whether the player is the kind of 
person who will make her work harder or easier. She has worked with 
both kinds.

### Secrets (GM Only)
Edda knows what Drenhold is. Not from Guild records — from the old 
woman who taught her Thorncraft, who had been there once before the 
Greymark expanded to cover it. She was told never to go back. She has 
been having dreams about the symbol the Wardens carved into the 
survivor's arm. She recognized it the moment she heard the description. 
It is a Thorncraft mark — one of the oldest ones. It means something 
is trying to communicate.
