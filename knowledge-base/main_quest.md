# Quest: The Shattered Seal

## Type
Investigation and survival — main story quest

## Hook
The morning after the player arrives in Greyhold, Guildmaster Aldric 
calls them into the Guild Hall privately. Not through the contract board. 
Privately. He looks like he has not slept.

He tells the player that a fourth outpost — Ashwatch Post — went silent 
two days ago. He is not sending another team. He is sending one person, 
quietly, without paperwork, because the last thing he needs is the Kingdom 
of Valdenmoor finding out the Guild has lost four outposts in six months 
and demanding control of Greyhold in exchange for military support.

He needs to know what happened. He needs to know if whatever happened is 
still out there. And he needs it done before anyone starts asking questions 
he cannot answer.

## Objectives

### Main Objective
Reach Ashwatch Post, determine what destroyed it, and return with 
actionable intelligence for the Guild.

### Optional Objectives
- Find out what happened to the two investigation teams Aldric sent before
- Recover the Ashwatch Post's intelligence logs if they still exist
- Determine whether the attacks are connected to the accelerating Riftings
- Return any survivors if there are any

## Key NPCs
- **Aldric Vane** — quest giver, knows more than he says
- **Torben Grall** — can brief the player on what Ashwatch Post was 
  like before it went silent, knew the Wardens stationed there personally
- **Signe the Thornwarden** — can provide Blight-resistance preparations 
  for the journey, warns the player that what she has been seeing in 
  recent survivors suggests something different is happening in the 
  Greymark
- **Kael Dunmore** — the rival, encountered at the edge of the Greymark 
  heading in the same direction, claims to be on independent contract work

## Location
Begins in Greyhold. Primary action in the Ashfringe and the Greymark 
beyond it, ending at Ashwatch Post approximately twelve miles into 
corrupted territory.

## The Three Clues

### Clue 1 — The Formation Tracks
Half a mile outside Greyhold's eastern gate, on the road toward Ashwatch 
Post, the player finds tracks. Not random creature movement. Organized 
tracks — multiple corrupted creatures moving in the same direction, in 
parallel lines, spaced evenly. Something directed them. Whatever it was, 
it knew where it was going.

### Clue 2 — The Survivor's Mark
In the ruins of a small waystation between Greyhold and Ashwatch Post, 
the player finds a Warden who was part of the first investigation team. 
She is alive but barely. She has been hiding for weeks. Before she loses 
consciousness she says one thing: they were not hunting. They were 
building something. She has a symbol carved into her forearm that she 
does not remember making — a circle with three lines through it. It is 
not a Warden mark. It is not a Runecraft symbol anyone in Greyhold 
recognizes.

### Clue 3 — Ashwatch Post Itself
The post was not destroyed. It was emptied. No bodies. No blood. No signs 
of a fight beyond one overturned table and a door torn off its hinges from 
the inside. The intelligence logs are gone. But carved into the stone wall 
of the main room, in the same symbol from the survivor's forearm, is a 
single word in old Thunorheim script: DRENHOLD.

Nobody currently alive knows what Drenhold is. It is not on any Guild map.

## The Twist
The corrupted creatures are not acting alone and they are not acting on 
pure instinct. There is something in the deep Greymark — something old, 
something that was there before the Blight spread this far — that is 
directing them. It is not hostile to humans by nature. It has been made 
hostile. Someone or something has been feeding it a specific signal, 
accelerating the Blight around it, and using the resulting Riftings as 
cover for something else entirely.

The Wardens from the missing outposts are not dead. They were taken to 
Drenhold. Why has not been established yet. That is the thread that 
pulls the campaign forward.

## Possible Outcomes

### Full Success
Player reaches Ashwatch Post, recovers the intelligence logs from a 
hidden cache, finds the survivor, identifies the symbol, and returns 
to Greyhold with all three clues documented. Aldric now has enough to 
act. The Guild begins a classified investigation. The player is the 
only field operative who knows what is actually happening.

**World state changes:** Aldric's trust level increases significantly. 
Guild reputation increases. The Drenhold thread opens as an active 
world flag. The survivor is evacuated to Greyhold.

### Partial Success
Player reaches the post, finds the symbol, but cannot recover the logs 
or find the survivor. Returns with the Drenhold lead but nothing else. 
Aldric is grateful but worried — one lead is not enough to justify 
action with the kingdoms watching.

**World state changes:** Aldric's trust increases moderately. Drenhold 
thread opens but flagged as unverified. Guild reputation increases 
slightly.

### Failure
Player is driven back by a Rifting before reaching Ashwatch Post, or 
reaches the post and is forced to retreat without gathering intelligence. 
Returns to Greyhold with nothing.

**World state changes:** No reputation change. Aldric's trust decreases 
slightly. The Drenhold thread does not open. A fifth outpost goes silent 
one week later.

### Catastrophic Failure
Player is captured or killed in the Greymark.

**World state changes:** Aldric sends no more solo operatives. The Guild 
begins official negotiations with Valdenmoor for military support. The 
kingdom's conditions are steep. The frontier changes politically in ways 
that make everything harder going forward.

## Secrets (GM Only)
- Kael Dunmore encountered on the road is not on independent contract 
  work. He was hired by a Valdenmoor noble to retrieve whatever 
  intelligence the Guild has been hiding about the Greymark situation. 
  He does not know about Drenhold yet. If the player finds it first he 
  will want that information.
- The symbol carved into the survivor's arm and the wall of Ashwatch 
  Post is not corrupted in origin. It is a very old Runecraft inscription 
  — pre-Guild, possibly pre-kingdom. Signe recognizes it if shown but 
  will not say so immediately. She needs time to think about what it means 
  that something in the Greymark knows old Runecraft.
- Drenhold is a pre-Blight settlement — a city that existed before the 
  Greymark expanded to cover it. It is not ruins. Parts of it are still 
  standing. Something has been maintaining it.

## Rewards
- Guild payment for completed contract
- Aldric's personal trust — opens access to classified Guild intelligence
- The Drenhold thread — the backbone of the main campaign
- The survivor's testimony if recovered — a named NPC with knowledge of 
  what the investigation teams encountered
