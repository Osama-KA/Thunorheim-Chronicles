# Location: Greyhold

## Type
Frontier guild town

## Region
The Ashfringe — the contested boundary zone between the last stable 
kingdoms and the edge of the Greymark. Greyhold sits at the furthest 
point of the frontier that anyone still calls civilized.

## Summary
Greyhold is the operational headquarters of the Warden's Guild and the 
last real settlement before the Greymark begins. It is not a pretty town. 
It was not built to be. Every structure is low, thick-walled, and built 
with the assumption that at some point something large and corrupted will 
try to get through it. The walls are reinforced with runestone carvings 
— old Guild inscriptions that have been re-etched so many times the stone 
is smooth from centuries of maintenance.

The town has roughly two thousand permanent residents and at any given 
time another three to five hundred transient Wardens, contract workers, 
merchants, and people who have nowhere else to go. It smells like 
cookfire smoke, tallow, and the faint mineral tang that locals call 
greydust — particles carried on the wind from the Greymark that nobody 
talks about inhaling.

Greyhold functions. It does not thrive. The people here are practical, 
direct, and quietly proud of the fact that they have not been overrun 
yet. Outsiders are tolerated. Weakness is not.

## What The Player Notices Arriving Here
The first thing is the wall — black stone, twice the height of a man, 
with Warden lanterns burning orange at every tower. The gate is always 
open during daylight but two armed Wardens check everyone coming in. 
Inside, the streets are wide enough for supply carts and the buildings 
are built close together for warmth. The Guild Hall dominates the center 
of town — a squat fortress of a building with the Guild sigil carved 
above the door: a sword and a runestone crossed over a circle.

The Ashen Flagon tavern sits directly across from the Guild Hall. It is 
always busy. It is the place where contracts get picked up, information 
gets traded, and Wardens drink before heading out and drink harder when 
they come back.

## Key NPCs Present
- **Aldric Vane** — Greyhold Guildmaster, runs the Guild Hall, assigns 
  contracts
- **Maren Ashveld** — proprietor of the Ashen Flagon, knows everything 
  that happens in Greyhold
- **Torben Grall** — senior Warden, veteran of fourteen Riftings, 
  unofficial mentor to new operatives
- **Signe the Thornwarden** — operates a small practice near the east 
  wall, controversial but quietly essential
- **Edric Fenn** — traveling merchant, always has supplies, asks too 
  many questions

## Current Tensions
- Three Warden outposts to the east have gone silent in the last six 
  months. The Guild has sent two investigation teams. Neither returned.
- A recent Rifting hit the village of Dunweld four miles south. Survivors 
  arrived in Greyhold three days ago. They are not talking about what 
  they saw.
- Guildmaster Aldric is under pressure from the Kingdom of Valdenmoor 
  to the west, which has begun demanding Guild intelligence reports in 
  exchange for continued financial support.

## Notable Locations Within Greyhold
- **The Guild Hall** — contracts, briefings, equipment requisition, 
  Guildmaster's office
- **The Ashen Flagon** — tavern, information hub, where the player 
  begins each session
- **Signe's Practice** — Thorncraft healing, controversial supplies, 
  information about the Blight
- **The Runestone Market** — small market where Runescribes sell 
  pre-inscribed items and materials
- **The Greywall Gate** — the eastern exit toward the Greymark, 
  guarded at all hours

## Secrets (GM Only)
- Guildmaster Aldric knows the three missing outposts were not lost to 
  a Rifting. The reports he received before communication cut off 
  described something organized — corrupted creatures moving in 
  formation, with purpose. He has told nobody.
- Maren Ashveld at the Ashen Flagon is a former Warden who retired 
  after losing two fingers to a corrupted wolf. She still carries a 
  blade and still knows how to use it.
- The greydust the locals inhale daily has long-term effects that Signe 
  is tracking quietly. The data is not good.
