# Factions

## Faction 1: The Warden's Guild — Ally

### Type
Cross-kingdom professional institution

### Public Goal
Protect frontier settlements from Blight corruption and Rifting surges 
by deploying trained operatives, maintaining intelligence networks, and 
coordinating emergency response across kingdom borders.

### True Goal
The same — but the Guild's senior leadership has begun to suspect that 
the current Rifting acceleration is not natural. Guildmaster Aldric and 
a small circle of senior Wardens are quietly investigating whether 
something is actively destabilizing the Greymark. They are doing this 
without informing the kingdoms because they do not trust what the 
kingdoms would do with that information.

### Leadership
**Aldric Vane — Guildmaster of Greyhold**
Late fifties. Former field Warden who retired to administration after 
a Rifting took his right hand at the wrist. Replaced it with a 
functional iron prosthetic that he maintains himself. Pragmatic, 
politically shrewd, genuinely committed to the Guild's mission in a 
way that occasionally makes him inflexible. He makes hard decisions 
without visible hesitation and carries the weight of them privately. 
He trusts very few people and extends that trust slowly. Once given 
it is not easily revoked.

### Structure
- Guildmaster (one per major outpost, Aldric holds Greyhold)
- Senior Wardens (veteran operatives with administrative authority)
- Licensed Wardens (active field operatives)
- Initiates (unlicensed operatives in their first year)
- Affiliated non-members (Thornwardens, contract scholars, merchants)

### Relationship To Player
The player operates under Guild license. The Guild is employer, 
information source, and the closest thing to institutional support 
the player has. Reputation with the Guild affects contract access, 
equipment availability, and what information Aldric shares privately.

### Relationship To Other Factions
- Kingdom of Valdenmoor: Strained. The Guild depends on kingdom 
  funding but resists kingdom control. Aldric is currently managing 
  Valdenmoor's demands for intelligence access while giving them as 
  little as possible.
- Thornwardens: Informal alliance. Not official Guild members but 
  tolerated and quietly relied upon. The relationship is pragmatic 
  on both sides.

### How They View The Player Based On Reputation
- Low reputation: Professional distance. Contracts are basic, 
  information is minimal, Aldric does not meet personally.
- Neutral reputation: Standard working relationship. Access to 
  Guild resources, briefings, and mid-tier contracts.
- High reputation: Aldric's personal trust. Access to classified 
  intelligence, priority contracts, and knowledge of what the Guild 
  actually suspects about the Greymark.

### Secrets (GM Only)
Aldric has a complete record of every outpost that has gone silent 
in the last three years — not just the four recent ones. The pattern 
goes back further than he has admitted publicly. He has a theory about 
what Drenhold is but will only share it with someone he trusts 
completely. The Guild's finances are in worse shape than anyone knows. 
Two more kingdoms have quietly stopped payments. The Guild has 
approximately eight months of operational funding left.

### Related Locations
Greyhold, Ashwatch Post, Guild outposts across the Ashfringe

### Related Quests
The Shattered Seal, all active Guild contracts

## Faction 2: The Kingdom of Valdenmoor — Opposing

### Type
Human kingdom, politically expansionist

### Public Goal
Maintain regional stability, protect Valdenmoor citizens, and ensure 
the Warden's Guild operates transparently and accountably as a 
recipient of kingdom funding.

### True Goal
Absorb the Warden's Guild into Valdenmoor's military structure. The 
kingdom's leadership views the Guild as an independent intelligence 
and military asset operating inside Valdenmoor's sphere of influence 
without Valdenmoor control. The current strategy is to use financial 
leverage to extract Guild intelligence, then use that intelligence 
to justify a formal takeover under the pretext of improved frontier 
management.

### Leadership
**Lord Castor Veld — Kingdom Envoy to the Ashfringe**
Mid forties. Educated, polished, and completely comfortable with the 
gap between what he says and what he means. Veld is not a soldier — 
he is an administrator and a political operator. He arrived in 
Greyhold six weeks ago ostensibly to discuss the Guild's funding 
arrangement. He has not left. He attends Guild briefings when invited 
and gathers information when not. He is unfailingly courteous to 
everyone including people he intends to ruin.

### Structure
- Kingdom Court (distant, in Valdenmoor's capital)
- Lord Envoy (Castor Veld, present in Greyhold)
- Kingdom Observers (three officials embedded in Greyhold, 
  nominally administrative)
- Valdenmoor Military (not present in Greyhold yet — this is 
  deliberate and temporary)

### Relationship To Player
Valdenmoor has no direct authority over the player. Veld will be 
courteous and subtly probing in any interaction. He wants to know 
what the player knows, what they are doing for Aldric, and whether 
they can be useful. If the player has information Valdenmoor wants 
he will offer payment. He will not threaten directly. He does not 
need to.

### Relationship To Other Factions
- Warden's Guild: Publicly supportive, privately adversarial. 
  Funding is the leash.
- Other Kingdoms: Valdenmoor is the largest and wealthiest kingdom 
  in the Ashfringe region. Other kingdoms watch it carefully and 
  cooperate minimally.

### How They View The Player Based On Reputation
- Low reputation with Guild, neutral with Valdenmoor: Veld sees 
  potential leverage. Will approach with an offer.
- High reputation with Guild: Veld views the player as an asset 
  worth cultivating or a threat worth monitoring depending on 
  player behavior.
- Player openly works against Valdenmoor: Veld becomes a background 
  obstacle — nothing overt, just doors that stop opening and 
  contracts that stop appearing.

### Secrets (GM Only)
Valdenmoor already has a spy inside the Guild — not Veld, someone 
closer to Aldric than Aldric knows. The kingdom's interest in the 
Greymark is not purely political. Someone in Valdenmoor's court has 
private knowledge of what Drenhold is and wants it kept quiet. 
Veld does not know this part. He is a tool being used by people 
above him who have not told him everything.

### Related Locations
Greyhold (Veld is present), Valdenmoor capital (distant)

### Related Quests
The Shattered Seal (opposing interest), any quest involving 
Guild intelligence
