# Artifact: The Ashen Seal

## Type
Runic artifact — ancient, pre-Guild origin

## Physical Description
A flat disc of black stone roughly the size of a palm, weight 
surprisingly light for its density. Three lines are carved through 
a circle on one face — the same symbol found on the survivor's arm 
and the wall of Ashwatch Post. The reverse face is smooth except 
for a single word in old Thunorheim script: DRENHOLD. The edges 
are worn smooth from handling. It is very old.

## Origin
Pre-dates the Warden's Guild by at least three centuries. Recovered 
by a Warden from a Blightseam excavation forty years ago and logged 
as an unidentified artifact in the Guild's archive. It sat in a 
storage chest in the Guild Hall basement until six months ago when 
it went missing from the archive without explanation.

## What It Does
The Seal does not function as a conventional runic artifact. It does 
not produce effects when activated. Instead it responds — when carried 
within the Greymark it grows faintly warm near active Blightseams and 
cold near whatever is directing the corrupted creatures. It is less 
a weapon and more a compass for something that does not appear on 
any map. Lysa the Runescribe would recognize its inscription style 
as pre-collegium runic tradition if she examined it.

## Who Wants It
- Whatever is in Drenhold — the warmth response suggests the Seal 
  was made to communicate with something there
- Valdenmoor — Castor Veld has a description of it from the spy 
  inside the Guild and has been quietly looking for it
- Lysa Vorn — would want to study it the moment she learned it existed

## Risks
Carrying the Seal inside the Greymark means being detectable. 
Whatever is directing the corrupted creatures can sense it. This 
cuts both ways — the Seal draws attention but also draws the 
creatures away from the player's exact position toward the Seal's 
location, which can be used tactically if the player is clever.

## Current Location
Unknown. Missing from the Guild archive for six months. Last person 
to access the archive before it disappeared was a Guild initiate 
who has since vanished from Greyhold without filing a departure notice.

## Secrets (GM Only)
The Seal was not made by humans. It predates human settlement in 
the Ashfringe region. Edda the Thornwarden's teacher mentioned it 
once by a different name — the Calling Stone. It was made in 
Drenhold, for Drenhold, and whatever is there recognizes it. 
Finding the Seal and bringing it to Drenhold is not a danger. 
It is an invitation.
